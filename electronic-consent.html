ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

Electronic Records and Consent

Important information about electronic records, notices and communications.

This page provides general information. It is not itself an electronic consent agreement and does not record or prove that a customer has consented to receive electronic records.

Electronic records and communications

If electronic delivery is offered, certain records or notices may be provided electronically where permitted by law and where the required consent has been obtained.

Access to records

Before obtaining consent, the operator should explain which records will be provided electronically, how they can be accessed, how long they will remain available and how a customer can request a paper copy.

Technical requirements

The actual consent process should explain the minimum device, software and accessibility requirements needed to access and retain the relevant records.

Withdrawing consent

Where applicable, customers should be informed how to withdraw electronic-delivery consent, when the withdrawal takes effect, and whether any consequences or fees apply as permitted by law.

Updates to contact information

Customers should be able to update their email address or other required contact information through a verified process.

Before you agree

Read the actual consent terms carefully. Do not assume that creating an account, verifying an email address or using this website alone constitutes consent to every type of electronic record or signature.

Questions

For questions about electronic communications, visit the contact page and use the verified support options available there.
