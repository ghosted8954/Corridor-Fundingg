Frequently asked questions

Find general information about applications, accounts and loan offers.

What is Corridor Funding?

Corridor Funding is a website designed to present financing information and provide an online experience for exploring available loan products. Actual services depend on verified product and jurisdiction availability.

What types of loans can I explore?

The website may present personal, mortgage, business and debt-consolidation products. Availability and eligibility vary. A product displayed on the website is not a guarantee that it is available to every applicant.

How do I start an application?

Review the relevant product information, check the applicable requirements, and use the application page if the product is available. Provide accurate information and review the applicable privacy notice and disclosures.

Does submitting an application guarantee approval?

No. Applications may be reviewed for eligibility and may require additional information. Submission does not guarantee approval, a particular rate, or funding.

How will I know if my application has been reviewed?

If customer account and application-status features are enabled, sign in to check your application status. You may also use the official support channel for questions.

What interest rate or fees will I pay?

Rates, fees, repayment terms and other costs depend on the actual product and offer. Review the written terms and required disclosures before accepting an offer.

Is an application fee required?

Do not assume that any fee is required. Verify every charge using independently confirmed official contact details and review the applicable written terms before paying anything.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password-reset option on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Email verification helps confirm access to the email address associated with an account. It does not establish identity, creditworthiness or loan eligibility.

How is my personal information handled?

Review the Privacy Policy to understand how information is collected, used, retained and protected. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or sensitive identity documents through an unverified communication channel.

What should I do if I have a complaint?

Visit the complaints page for the available complaint process. Keep copies of relevant communications and avoid sending sensitive information until you have verified the official channel.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page to see the contact methods currently configured for this website. Response times depend on actual support availability.
