CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Complaints and concerns

We encourage customers to raise concerns so they can be reviewed through an appropriate and documented process.

How to raise a complaint

  1. Describe the issue clearly and include relevant dates.
  2. Include a reference number if one has been provided.
  3. Explain what outcome or resolution you are seeking.
  4. Keep copies of your communications for your records.

Submit your concern

Use the website's contact or support page to find the available submission process. Sign in first if the support feature requires an account.

Contact Support

What happens next?

Your complaint should be recorded, reviewed by an authorized person, and handled according to the applicable complaint process. Any response time or escalation procedure must be confirmed and published by the actual website operator.

Keep your information safe

Do not include passwords, complete payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents in your complaint. Use only verified official contact channels.

External complaint options

Depending on the actual provider, product and jurisdiction, a complaint may be eligible for review by a relevant regulator or consumer-protection agency. Verify the appropriate agency and current complaint procedure for your circumstances.

This page describes a general complaint process. It does not claim that Corridor Funding is licensed, affiliated with a regulator, or authorized to offer every product in every state.
