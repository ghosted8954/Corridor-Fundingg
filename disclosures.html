IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Financing Disclosures

Review the following information before submitting an application or accepting an offer.

1. No guarantee of approval

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, a specific loan amount, a particular interest rate or funding. Any decision depends on applicable requirements, verification and review.

2. Availability by state and product

Product availability depends on the applicable jurisdiction, operating entity, product requirements and any required authorization. A state or product appearing on this website does not establish that the product is legally available there.

Availability must be confirmed before an application is accepted or a product is offered to a customer.

3. Interest rates, fees and repayment

Rates, fees, repayment schedules, total borrowing costs and other terms vary by product and applicant. Review the actual written offer and all required disclosures before agreeing to any loan.

A calculator or example on this website is an estimate only. It may not include all applicable fees, taxes, insurance or other costs.

4. Funding times

Processing and funding times may vary. Do not rely on a specific funding date unless it is confirmed in the applicable agreement or by a verified authorized representative.

5. Credit and identity checks

Any credit report, identity verification or other eligibility check must be disclosed and performed only as authorized and permitted by applicable law. This website must not claim a check has occurred unless it actually has.

6. Electronic communications

Electronic disclosures, notices and signatures may be subject to specific consent requirements. Review the Electronic Consent page for information about the electronic delivery process, once that process has been verified and implemented.

7. Privacy and information security

Review the Privacy Policy to understand how personal information may be handled. Only submit sensitive information through a verified channel designed to protect that information.

8. Complaints and assistance

For questions or concerns, use the verified contact methods published on this website. The complaints page should explain the actual complaint process and any applicable escalation options.

9. Important warning about payments

Verify all fees, payment instructions and account details through independently confirmed official channels. Do not treat a message, application status or unverified payment request as proof that a loan has been approved or funded.

10. Legal and licensing information

The operating entity should publish accurate legal identity, licensing and jurisdiction-specific information where required. Do not interpret this page as a representation that Corridor Funding is licensed in every U.S. state.

General website information does not replace the final loan agreement or disclosures required by applicable law. These disclosures must be reviewed and adapted to the actual business, products and jurisdictions before the website is launched.
