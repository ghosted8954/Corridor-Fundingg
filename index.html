COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Some useful things to understand before starting.

Does starting an application guarantee approval?

No. Starting or submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Decisions depend on the applicable product, eligibility requirements, verification, and underwriting.

Are financing products available in every state?

Not necessarily. Availability depends on the product and the applicable state authorization and licensing requirements. Availability must be verified before accepting an application.

Will I know the terms before accepting an offer?

Applicable offer terms and disclosures should be presented for review before acceptance. Review the APR, fees, repayment obligations, conditions, and other applicable terms carefully.

Can I track an application online?

Account-based application tracking is part of the planned platform. You will need to sign in to view application information when the relevant account features are configured and available.
