THE APPLICATION PROCESS

Understand each step before you apply

The exact process depends on the product and your circumstances. These are general steps, not a promise of approval or funding.

01

Explore available options

Review the products and jurisdictions currently available. Check the eligibility information and applicable disclosures.

02

Create an account

If registration is available, create an account and verify your email address to access the customer area.

03

Complete an application

Provide accurate information requested by the application. Only submit information through authorized, secure channels.

04

Application review

An application may require further information or review. Submission does not mean the application has been approved.

05

Review any offer carefully

If an offer is made, review the amount, interest rate, fees, repayment schedule, conditions and applicable disclosures.

06

Next steps

Any disbursement or repayment arrangements depend on the verified agreement and the relevant product's actual process.

Before you proceed

Never pay an unexpected fee or transfer money based solely on a message claiming that a loan is approved. Confirm any charges, contractual terms and payment instructions through independently verified official channels.

Availability, eligibility, rates, fees and funding times vary. Not all applicants qualify, and approval is not guaranteed.
